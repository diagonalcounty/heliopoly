# App Store archive (one app)

Archive the **Heliopoly** scheme only.

- Bundle ID: `heliopoly.live.Heliopoly`
- Destinations: iPhone + iPad (universal)
- Do **not** archive any `HeliopolyPhone` target (prototype / wrong bundle). That listing is not the App Store app.

In Xcode: scheme menu → **Heliopoly** → Product → Archive. Confirm Organizer shows bundle `heliopoly.live.Heliopoly` before Upload.
