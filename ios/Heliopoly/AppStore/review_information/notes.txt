GUIDELINE 2.1 — APP REVIEW INFORMATION (Heliopoly 1.5.0)

Heliopoly is a complete, offline, single-player solar-system strategy game for iPhone and iPad. A SwiftUI shell loads the bundled HTML/JS game from the app package over a custom URL scheme (heliopoly://). Core play does not require a network connection, account, or any external service.

3) FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Heliopoly is a turn-based strategy / board game. The app opens on three doors. Journey is the full game: the player names a rocket, chooses methane or hydrogen propellant, and competes against on-device AI rivals from Mercury to the Saturn moons. Players buy planetary claims, plant fuel depots, manage cash on the in-game ledger, and win by being the last solvent rocket flying. There is no turn cap. Arcade holds one-minute toys that do not change a game in progress. Lab holds experiments.

Target audience: players of offline strategy and classic property/economy board games who want a space-themed expedition.

Problem / value: a complete, account-free, offline strategy game with bundled rules (Helios Ops Manual) and four AI difficulty levels. Open the app and play.

4) HOW TO ACCESS MAIN FEATURES
No login. No demo account. No sample files. Airplane Mode is fine.

Review path:
1. Open the app, you land on three doors, tap Journey, Launch. (Journey shows the New game panel: name any rocket or leave the default, choose Methane, pick Total pilots 3 for a short session, then tap Launch.)
2. In Pilot Controls: tap Roll. Optionally use Break − / + to land short. The ship moves. If the world is for sale, tap Buy. Tap End turn. AI seats play automatically.
3. Tap Ops Manual / Book (header) for rules, glossary, and rival-rocket civilopedia. Dismiss to return to play.
4. If two rockets meet on a blank lane, Gravity Duel appears: choose Low or High, then roll.
5. Home (header) returns to the doors without quitting. On a fresh install Lab shows “Play one Arcade toy” and unlocks after one Arcade toy.

A few human turns are enough to review setup, board, claims, and AI. A full game is not required.
