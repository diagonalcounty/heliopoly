GUIDELINE 2.1 — APP REVIEW INFORMATION (Heliopoly 1.0.0)

Heliopoly is a complete, offline, single-player solar-system strategy game for iPad. It is not a website wrapper. A SwiftUI shell loads a bundled HTML/JS game from the app package over a custom URL scheme (heliopoly://). Core play does not require a network connection, account, or any external service.

3) FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Heliopoly is a turn-based strategy / board game. The player names a rocket, chooses methane or hydrogen propellant, and competes against on-device AI rivals from Mercury to the Saturn moons. Players buy planetary claims, plant fuel depots, manage cash on the in-game ledger, and win by being the last solvent rocket flying. There is no turn cap.

Target audience: players of offline strategy and classic property/economy board games who want a space-themed expedition.

Problem / value: a complete, account-free, offline strategy game with bundled rules (Helios Ops Manual) and four AI difficulty levels. Open the app and play.

4) HOW TO ACCESS MAIN FEATURES
No login. No demo account. No sample files. Airplane Mode is fine.

Review path:
1. Launch Heliopoly. The New game panel is visible immediately.
2. Leave “Seat 1 is human” checked. Name any rocket (or leave the default). Choose Methane. Set AI difficulty to Easy. Set Total pilots to 3 for a short session.
3. Tap Launch.
4. In Pilot Controls: tap Roll. Optionally use Break − / + to land short. The ship moves. If the world is for sale, tap Buy. Tap End turn. AI seats play automatically.
5. Tap Ops Manual (header) for rules, glossary, and rival-rocket civilopedia. Dismiss to return to play.
6. If two rockets meet on a blank lane, Gravity Duel appears: choose Low or High, then roll.

A few human turns are enough to review setup, board, claims, and AI. A full game is not required. Ignore “Self-play burst” unless you want an AI-only spectator demo.
