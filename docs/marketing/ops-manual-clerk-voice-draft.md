## “The ledger & Angzarr (⍼)” — draft

> On old Earth, public ledgers recorded who paid whom. Those books did not survive quantum machines.
>
> Currency on the Mainline is **Angzarr (⍼)**. You will see **⍼** before the amount (example: ⍼150). Angzarr is post-quantum digital currency. It still requires a ledger.
>
> The book is the **AIL** — Automated Interplanetary Asset Ledger. It records:
>
> 1. **Contracts** — ownership, rent owed, fuel paid.  
> 2. **History** — expeditions, crashes, names that lasted.
>
> If the ledger drops your deed, the claim returns to the bank. Starting cash is not a glitch. It is your first line — a funded launch.

## “The Mainline” — draft

> Rockets fly one path. That path is the **Mainline**. You do not select a shortcut.
>
> Circuit order: Earth → Venus → Mercury → Mars system (Elon, Mars, Phobos, Deimos) → asteroid belt blanks → Jupiter (Holst + moons) → Saturn (Daktulios + moons) → home to Earth.
>
> One full loop home is a **rotation**.
>
> Blank lanes cost no leave fuel. They are not safe. Another rocket already present means a **Gravity Duel**.

---

## “How to win” — draft

> **Last rocket flying wins.** Others exit by bankruptcy, stranding, or abandonment.
>
> The ledger then writes your name into history as one of the **greatest of all kind**.
>
> There is no round limit. There is no “enough Angzarr.” Last standing is the record.
>
> When a rocket leaves, its deeds return to the **bank**. Fuel depots on those deeds are removed.
>
> See **Glossary** for turn / round / rotation. Ledger events use **rounds**.
