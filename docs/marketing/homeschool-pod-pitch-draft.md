## One-pager (web / parent email) — proposed

### Who it’s for

Homeschool pods and co-ops that want kids arguing about fuel, rent, and insolvency — while practicing scarcity, trade, and systems thinking — without turning play into a quiz.

### What it is

**Heliopoly** is orbital economics on the Mainline. Pilots buy claims, burn propellant, duel on blank lanes, and try to be the last rocket flying. Money is **Angzarr (⍼)**. The Ops Manual reads like a clerk’s memo, not a textbook.

### First session (~20 minutes)

1. **2 min** — Open Ops Manual → “Read this first” + “How to win.” Last rocket flying; no timer.  
2. **3 min** — Show the ledger: cash is ⍼; nothing counts until the book says so.  
3. **12 min** — One short expedition: 2–3 human seats + AI fillers (or hot-seat around one device). Pause on first rent, first fuel leave, first Gravity Duel.  
4. **3 min** — Debrief questions (facilitator, not in-game):  
   - What ran out first — cash or fuel?  
   - Who owned the tollbooth (hubs)?  
   - Would you park or keep moving?

### What kids practice (honest claims)

| Practice | In play |
|----------|---------|
| Scarcity | Propellant to leave wells; cash for rent |
| Tradeoffs | CH₄ vs H₂; buy vs hold cash; duel risk |
| Systems | Full-system ownership doubles rent; hubs stack |
| Record-keeping | Ledger / AIL as the source of truth |

**Do not claim:** grade-level standards alignment, “educational app” certification, or curriculum replacement.

### Logistics

- Offline after download (shipped game package).  
- Browser free play: https://heliopoly.live/ (same rules engine).  
- Open source (MIT).  
- Age rating: board competition / rivalry — keep questionnaire honest.
